- `param.v`: preliminaries for parametricity
- `monalg_ext.v`: extension of `monalg.v` of the multinomials library
- `genquote.v`: automatic generation of reification procedure
- `syntax.v`: reified syntax for ring and module expressions
- `cring_base.v`: computation-oriented representation of commutative polynomials
- `cring_refinements.v`: refinements for commutative polynomials
- `ncring_base.v`: computation-oriented representation of non-commutative
  polynomials
- `ring_base.v`: computation-oriented representation of mixed non-commutative
  and commutative polynomials
- `module_base.v`: computation-oriented representation of free modules (over
  polynomials)
